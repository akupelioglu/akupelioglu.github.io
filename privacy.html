LifeOS privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

LifeOS is a personal tool used only by its owner, Arda Kupelioglu. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, LifeOS uses read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) to read emails from a fixed list of billing senders, such as a broadband provider, a mobile provider, an insurer and a bank's payment notifications. It cannot send, change or delete email.

What it stores

From those emails it extracts the provider, amount, due or payment date and a link back to the email, and writes them to the owner's private Notion workspace. It does not keep copies of email content anywhere else. Access tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub repository secrets and never published.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or for training AI models. It is only processed by the services needed to run the tool: Google (Gmail API), GitHub (where the daily job runs) and Notion (where results are stored).

LifeOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
