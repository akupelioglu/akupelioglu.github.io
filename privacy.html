Privacy policy

LifeOS is a private tool used only by its owner. It has read-only access to the owner's own Gmail account and stores what it reads only in the owner's private workspace. Data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

LifeOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
