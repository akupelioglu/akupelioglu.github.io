LifeOS

LifeOS is a personal tool used only by its owner, Arda Kupelioglu. Once a day it reads bill, renewal and payment emails from a short list of known senders in the owner's own Gmail account, and records the provider, amount and due date in the owner's private Notion workspace.

It is not offered to anyone else and has no sign-up.
